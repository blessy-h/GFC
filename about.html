OUR STORY

More Than Just Good Food.

Grand Festive Cafe (GFC) is a welcoming restaurant in Udayarvilai, Kanyakumari, created for food lovers who enjoy delicious food, refreshing beverages, and memorable moments with family and friends. Our menu brings together a variety of flavorful choices, including biryani, grilled dishes, pizza, wraps, fast food, and refreshing drinks.

Every dish is prepared with attention to taste and quality, giving our guests a satisfying dining experience. Located in the Kanyakumari region, GFC is a convenient destination for those looking for a restaurant near Udayar Vilai, delicious food near Colachel, or a comfortable place to enjoy a meal with loved ones. Whether you are meeting friends, spending time with family, enjoying a quick bite, or simply looking for great food, Grand Festive Cafe is a place where good food and great moments come together.
