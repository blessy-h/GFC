OUR STORY

More Than Just Good Food.

Grand Festive Cafe (GFC Restaurant) is a welcoming dining space in Udayar Vilai, Kanyakumari, created for food lovers who enjoy flavourful non-veg dishes, satisfying meals, and memorable times with family and friends. Our kitchen specializes in rich chicken dum biryani, smoky grilled food, freshly baked pizzas, crispy fast food wraps, and refreshing cafe beverages.

Every dish is prepared with dedication to authentic taste and quality ingredients, creating a consistently satisfying dining experience. Situated conveniently in the Kanyakumari district, GFC Restaurant is a favoured destination whether you are searching for a restaurant near me, looking for a dependable nearby restaurant around Udayar Vilai, or traveling from nearby towns like Colachel, Marthandam, or Nagercoil.

Whether you are catching up with friends over snacks, hosting a family dinner, or seeking recommended restaurants near me for appetizing non-veg cuisine, GFC Restaurant welcomes you with genuine warmth and great flavours.
