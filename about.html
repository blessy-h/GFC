OUR STORY

More Than Just Good Food.

GFC Restaurant is a welcoming food destination in Udayar Vilai, Kanyakumari, created for people who love delicious food and good company.

From flavorful biryanis and smoky grills to pizzas, wraps and refreshing beverages, every dish is prepared with care and attention to flavour.

Whether you are enjoying a meal with family, meeting friends or simply treating yourself, we want every visit to feel special.
