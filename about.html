OUR STORY

A Welcoming Place in Udayar Vilai

GFC Restaurant was created with a clear and humble passion: to provide a vibrant, comfortable dining space where residents and visitors in Kanyakumari can gather over memorable food.

From our kitchen counter to our dining tables, we believe that dining out should always feel special. We focus on culinary craftsmanship, hospitable service, and food that brings people closer together.

OUR FOOD

Honest Flavours & Quality Cooking

Every dish that leaves our kitchen starts with carefully sourced ingredients, balanced spices, and strict culinary standards. Whether it is our slow-dum biryanis infused with fragrant herbs, our smoky tandoori grills, or freshly baked thin crust pizzas, we take pride in preparing food to order.

We never cut corners on freshness, cleanliness, or taste. Our menu combines the best of traditional Indian warmth with popular cafe favourites, creating a balanced selection that satisfies every family member.
